# deepseek-v3-paper-released

DeepSeek-V3 introduces a 671B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 37B active parameters per token. The architecture builds explicitly on prior MoE work — Switch Transformer and GLaM are cited as direct ancestors. The paper revives interest in the moe-architecture lineage.
